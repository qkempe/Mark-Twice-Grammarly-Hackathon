Should Homework Be Limited?

Many students spend three hours a night on homework. This leaves little time for sleep or family.

Teachers assign homework to practice skills from class. This is important, but the amount matters.

Studies show that homework helps high school students more than younger kids. This means elementary schools could assign less.

Some parents worry their children will fall behind. This concern is understandable. Others say kids need time to play.

Schools could limit homework to one hour a night. This would protect sleep and still allow practice. That approach balances both goals.
