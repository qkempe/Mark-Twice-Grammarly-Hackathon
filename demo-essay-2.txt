Phones Belong in Lockers

Many schools are banning phones during class. Supporters say phones distract students, they point to falling test scores.

Although phones can be useful tools, most students use them for games and social media. A single notification can pull attention away for several minutes, it takes time to refocus.

Some teachers use phones for quick quizzes, and students enjoy them. Still, the research is mixed; therefore, schools should move carefully.

A clear policy helps everyone, students know what to expect. Because the rules are the same in every room, there are fewer arguments.
