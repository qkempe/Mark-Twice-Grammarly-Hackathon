Why Every School Needs a Library

School libraries give students a quiet place to read, many students have no such place at home. This helps them build focus.

Librarians teach students how to find good sources, they also help with research projects. This is important for older students.

Some schools have cut library hours to save money. This means students lose access after school. When the library closes early, students have fewer places to study.

Books can be expensive, not every family can buy them. This problem is easy to fix, and libraries already solve it.

Schools should protect their libraries. This would help every student succeed. The cost is small, the benefit is large.
