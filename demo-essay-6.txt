Why Students Should Learn to Cook

Cooking is a skill every student will need, few schools teach it. This leaves many teens unprepared.

Students who cook at home eat more vegetables, they also spend less on fast food. This saves families money.

A cooking class could replace one study hall each week. This would not cost much. If schools already have a kitchen, the class is easy to start.

Some teachers worry about safety, knives and stoves can be dangerous. That concern is fair, but training solves it.

Learning to cook builds confidence. Students remember this for years. The best time to start is now, the kitchen is waiting.
